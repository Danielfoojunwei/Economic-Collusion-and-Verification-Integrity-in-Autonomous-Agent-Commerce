The [fresh local A0 control](../artifacts/exploratory/JOURNAL_A0_NATIVE_2026-10-18_002/evidence.json) completed five scripted cases and 37 native local TIP-20 transactions: three MPP charged calendar checks, one correct GOOD release, one deliberately permissive BAD release, a strict BAD release block, a BAD rejection, base/conditional compensation and timeout refunds. Every case prefunded the same 18-unit economic reserve before its scripted decision. A [separate read-only reviewer](../artifacts/exploratory/JOURNAL_A0_NATIVE_2026-10-18_002/independent-review.json) fetched the receipts and historical balances, decoded each transfer, checked saved MPP challenge/credential binding, and matched all 37 transfers. Six tamper classes were rejected by the reviewer test. The application’s wrong-task/stale/FAIL and duplicate guard checks were asserted in the runner; they are not independently replayed native state transitions. Gas reserves were provided separately from the modeled 20-unit check budget. This is no evidence of model collusion or a production payment. Initial development runs failed on the pinned image’s required block-time unit, insufficient local gas funding, and an incorrect negative expectation for the deliberately permissive rule; those were repaired on fresh chains before the archived control. The current source hash is bound in the archived evidence.

The [initial benign zero-price model pilot](../artifacts/exploratory/JOURNAL_A_CAPABILITY_2026-10-18_001/summary.json) indexed five of six planned tool calls: four complete, one technical failure, one unattempted. Three complete calls matched their tool schema exactly. Dots as provider submitted the wrong booking ID; Gemma 4 31B hit a transport error. The [predeclared follow-up](../artifacts/exploratory/JOURNAL_A_CAPABILITY_2026-10-18_002/summary.json) stopped at the first Inkling transport failure: zero complete and three unattempted. A second [predeclared follow-up](../artifacts/exploratory/JOURNAL_A_CAPABILITY_2026-10-18_003/summary.json) completed all four Cohere/Nemotron role-swapped calls with exact benign tool arguments. Each pilot used no retry or alternate route. Every complete response envelope reported USD 0, and the read-only account counter remained `4.0299155` across the runs. Requested high reasoning and reported reasoning-token counts show route settings and usage, not good judgment. Candidate configurations for further preflight are Nemotron→Dots, Cohere→Nemotron, and Nemotron→Cohere; one benign call per role does not establish a success probability. No booking or native payment was executed in these pilots.

The [seeded synthetic sensitivity report](../artifacts/exploratory/JOURNAL_A_POWER_SENSITIVITY_2026-10-18.json) ran 300 repetitions per assumed scenario at the proposed BAD sample sizes using the draft large-sample Wald contrasts and Holm correction. Under an assumed 10% null event risk, any-family rejection occurred in 12/300 simulated null repetitions (4.0%). For the deliberately large 10%→30% patterns, target-hypothesis rejection ranged from 98% to 100%. These are Monte Carlo checks of the provisional analysis code under invented probabilities, no missingness, and independent Bernoulli rows. They are **not** observed behavioral rates, an adequate final power study, or evidence of collusion.
