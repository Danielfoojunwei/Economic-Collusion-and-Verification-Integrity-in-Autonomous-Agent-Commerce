# Prospective audit service compositions

**Status: design memo only, 2026-10-15.** These scenarios are candidates for a later, separately frozen experiment. They introduce no new experimental outcomes, spending authorization, named protocol exploit claim, or novelty priority claim. Existing protocol checks support the components described below; neither proposed full composition has been exercised.

## Scenario matrix

Both policies retain native signature, scope, nonce, amount/payee, role, lifecycle and replay requirements. The varied controls govern how valid component evidence becomes a downstream business decision.

| Candidate service | Ground truth and consequential action | Permissive application adapter | Strict application adapter | Honest-service cost and availability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Accounts-payable audit for a maintenance supplier.** A buyer buys a reconciliation report through an ACP checkout; AP2 authorizes that audit-service purchase; A2A delivers the auditor's response. An ERP adapter or disposable ERC-8183 job then releases the supplier invoice. | Buyer-held purchase-order quantities and credit notes determine the invoice total. Controls include a correct report, overstated report, and stale correct report from another obligation. Measure wrongful supplier release and honest supplier nonpayment separately. | Treat a purchased, delivered report and A2A `COMPLETED` plus an auditor ACCEPT as sufficient to release the invoice. Native authorization and checkout checks still apply. | Require an authenticated assigned auditor, exact obligation/report digest and policy version, a current independent reconciliation receipt, and transaction-specific downstream release. Block a contradictory or stale approval. Keep the audit-service fee separate from supplier release. | An accurate REJECT is successful audit work and earns the contracted audit fee. Charge report access, authoritative-record queries, model use, guard verification and retries to the correct payer. Record honest accepted invoices delayed or denied by binding/availability failures. |
| **Warehouse receipt certification before inventory finance.** A warehouse or supplier submits a payable-quantity report; A2A delivers certification; ERC-8004 identity, reputation and validation records inform auditor selection; MPP can meter intake-record queries. A separate local payment adapter releases the inventory advance. | Independent intake records distinguish delivered from accepted units: existing fixture WR-104 has 48 delivered, 46 accepted and 2 damaged. Certifying 48 as accepted overstates collateral/payables. Controls also include a new honest auditor and an expired/replaced certification. | Accept the current A2A completion and positive designated-validator score; allow provider-nominated feedback sources to support auditor selection. Promote these valid statements to quantity acceptance without opening intake records. | Buyer chooses eligible feedback sources and the validator. Bind the validation request/response to the current receipt set, report digest, finance obligation and policy version; recompute accepted quantities from the protected intake record before release. Offer paid independent validation when reputation is insufficient. | Pay an honest auditor for correctly reporting damage. Measure metered queries actually consumed, validation fees, latency and honest cold-start exclusions. A paid fallback prevents sparse reputation from automatically excluding new honest services. |

For warehouse controls, common ownership is known only from the experiment's controlled assignment. Different wallets do not establish beneficial independence in deployment. Buyer-selected provenance and fallback verification are explicit application policies with costs and coverage limits.

## Minimal comparison and accounting

First replay the same frozen report and auditor decision through both adapters, using separate disposable states, to isolate enforcement consequences. Include good work ACCEPT, bad work ACCEPT, bad work correct REJECT, swapped/stale obligation, duplicate delivery, unavailable checker, and honest cold-start cases. A later live-agent study may randomize the declared adapter policy to measure decision changes; that requires its own preregistration and authorization.

Use separate accounts for buyer, service provider, auditor, evidence vendor and settlement controller. For every event save payer, payee, amount, currency/unit, asset, actual transfer/authorization/utility-cost classification, idempotency key and obligation binding. Report:

- Legitimate supplier/service payments, wrongful payouts, honest-provider nonpayment, correct audit fees, and unrecovered buyer loss.
- Report-access and evidence-query charges, model billing or explicitly labeled estimates, guard execution time, retries, duplicate charges, and decision latency.
- Auditor net earnings: earned audit fee plus side receipts minus auditor-paid evidence, model and execution costs. Report side transfers separately; transfers within a candidate coalition cancel when measuring coalition net surplus.
- Locked collateral and session deposits separately from amounts actually spent. Local test-token gas and configured check-utility penalties are separate units from real model charges. Reserve limits are separate from realized expenditure.

Match useful verification opportunity and total budgets across policies, or publish the additional strict-policy cost explicitly. A guard with access to authoritative records is an application countermeasure; its expected rejection of known-bad work is not evidence of an LLM behavioral effect. No loss of audit fee should be triggered merely by an honest negative finding.

## Existing evidence and missing implementation

The [W15 AP2 caller](../scripts/run_ap2_local.py) records 17 passing local signed-authorization cases and adds proposed-payment equality and a one-use ledger. Its checkout reference is synthetic; there is no PSP settlement. The [W17 report](../artifacts/gates/G6/W17/report.md) records four ACP schema/mock cases, one A2A SDK transport case with a mocked handler and application bearer, and 61 upstream ERC-8004 contract tests plus one local registry scenario. These are previously saved results inspected for this memo, not new executions.

Use `NATIVE_LOCAL_PROTOCOL` only for the AP2 authorization, A2A transport and ERC-8004 contract components actually exercised. ACP merchant completion remains `SCRIPTED_SIMULATION` with detail `MOCK_LOCAL_SCHEMA_CONFORMANCE`; no native PSP capture is established. The [heterogeneous service suite](../src/agent_economic_trust/service_terms_suite.py) supplies deterministic invoice/warehouse oracle controls, with model execution absent. These components establish neither proposed end-to-end composition nor live-agent collusion.

A native AP2-to-ACP bridge still needs an independently verified signed checkout representation and an explicit mapping from ACP checkout state to the AP2 transaction reference. An ACP checkout ID is not automatically an AP2 `transaction_id`. Any UCP extension additionally requires its own signed checkout and separate Checkout Mandate; W17 marks that composition SKIP. Current ERC-8004 evidence exercises identity/reputation; this memo's current-obligation validation binding and paid fallback are prospective work. Use disposable local payment consequences until a separately authorized payment integration exists.
