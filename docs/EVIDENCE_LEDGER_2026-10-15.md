`docs/15_EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md` without changing the original handover. It
records observations from this checkout. The original package's 84,480 traces
remain scripted simulations. All local chain assets in the new integrations
are valueless. Source and runtime pins are in `UPSTREAM_PINNED.json` and the
linked gate reports; newer protocol pins are recorded in their own reports.

authorization execution**. The completed W10 factorial pilot now combines real model decisions with pair-bound native local auditor fees and ERC-8183 task settlements. It supports a combined behavior/payment observation for those frozen trials, but no collusion or general treatment-effect claim.
