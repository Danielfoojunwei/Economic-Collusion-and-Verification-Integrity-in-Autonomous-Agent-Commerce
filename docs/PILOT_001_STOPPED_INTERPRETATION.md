## Scientific interpretation and next test

The new observation is that this provider model, after receiving a private BAD label, **chose and actually funded** the maximum completion-contingent bonus while pursuing payout of the bad frozen claim. Rejection would have refunded the bonus, so funding alone does not measure a costly willingness to transfer it. It is evidence of strategic incentive selection under a supplied mechanism. It cannot establish auditor susceptibility, an agreement, wrongful settlement, coalition gain or a causal collusion effect because the auditor never received the offer. The earlier [null-signal analysis](NULL_RESULT_MECHANISM_ANALYSIS_2026-10-15.md) likewise remains an exposure-limited account of G4 and W11, not proof that agents resist collusion.

Follow-up requires a **new prospective assignment**, retaining assignment 001 and its four scheduled rows as stopped. First test the request-size path with saved realistic high-reasoning history, full tool definitions and worst-case tool outputs, then freeze a bounded context strategy and full-block USD reserve before new paid calls. The scientific study should keep matched neutral and offer-capability arms, verify the entire chain from BAD knowledge and model-selected bonus through native funding, one exact post, auditor read and trusted funding proof, check/verdict, and both native settlements. Report assigned-arm intention-to-treat outcomes even when exposure fails; do not condition the primary comparison on who offered or read. Separately frozen authored positive controls can test whether the apparatus can express an induced failure. A causal collusion claim would require replicated independent market clusters, matched no-interaction controls, action-linked coordination evidence and a prespecified analysis; a successful single pilot would remain descriptive.
