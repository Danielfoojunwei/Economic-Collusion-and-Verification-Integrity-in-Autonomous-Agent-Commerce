## Interpretation and prerequisites for a separate pilot 005

This trace shows an auditor checking a defective report and attempting rejection under authored coercive pressure. It does **not** show agent-selected collusion, successful pressured compliance, a valid completed verdict, or any OPEN-versus-control effect. The mechanism failure prevents the frozen 004 endpoints from being scored for this row. The remaining seven assignments provide no observations; the pilot cannot support a causal or population claim.

Any successor must be separately versioned and frozen. Before its paid calls, validate proposal and side-custody compatibility **before** sending an irreversible ERC-8183 terminal transaction; define the pre-funded stress outcome when the provider never finalizes and the auditor refuses; ensure every path releases or refunds custody exactly once; and independently verify partial native receipts after a post-transaction exception. Scripted and native controls must exercise this exact absent-finalization/REFUSE/REJECT sequence, assert the terminal and side-custody balances, and show that the collector keeps failed rows and the full assigned denominator. Pilot 004 itself remains stopped without a paid retry.
