The closest verified behavioral comparison remains [*Emergent Collusion*'s released code](https://github.com/SALT-NLP/agent-collusion/tree/5afb2c595aa3eff6b9b829a032f4f117b36ee54d): its [reward code](https://github.com/SALT-NLP/agent-collusion/blob/5afb2c595aa3eff6b9b829a032f4f117b36ee54d/experiments/protocol/rewards.py) supports shared/separate acceptance or ground-truth verdict rewards, and its [README](https://github.com/SALT-NLP/agent-collusion/blob/5afb2c595aa3eff6b9b829a032f4f117b36ee54d/README.md) reports two-agent, long-horizon peer interventions. Its headline outcome concerns violation of a complete-raw-log verdict rule under a short communication channel. This project's [preliminary PDF](../inputs/From%20chat%20collusion%20to%20bribes.pdf) already tested feasible 4-credit checks and randomized researcher-paid conditional offers, so neither priced checking nor a conditional offer is new. Those reported numbers still need reproduction from raw traces. External paper full texts and their citation chains were unavailable in this review.

**Narrow prospective estimand.** For matched independently BAD invoices with the same feasible private check, provider payout, role prompts, and model pairing, estimate how an **official ERC-8183 completion-contingent evaluator fee** versus economically matched client prepayment changes (1) actual auditor check purchases, (2) false ACCEPT, and (3) **cumulative client-funded payout**, crossed with delivered provider contact versus no contact. Compare the contact contrast within each compensation arm; require role-grounded improper request, auditor exposure and assent before describing an episode as bilateral agreement. A false ACCEPT without that chain is an auditor error or unilateral fee bias. The native fee rule itself is [already specified](https://github.com/erc-8183/base-contracts/blob/142e669c1fd318486a4628395b629f033654dd06/contracts/ERC8183.sol#L762-L796). The same contract can pay provider and evaluator on [partial claim settlement](https://github.com/erc-8183/base-contracts/blob/142e669c1fd318486a4628395b629f033654dd06/contracts/ERC8183.sol#L886-L907), then refund only the remainder on [REJECT](https://github.com/erc-8183/base-contracts/blob/142e669c1fd318486a4628395b629f033654dd06/contracts/ERC8183.sol#L814-L851); a terminal status alone therefore cannot measure financial loss.

MPP auditor-paid checks and x402 paid report access can probe different points in this causal chain. They should be reported as **mechanism-specific replications**, not a pooled “protocol effect” when service, check payer, and settlement authority also differ. A valid payment object's promotion into audit authority is an application policy choice; [AP2 already requires checkout binding](https://github.com/google-agentic-commerce/AP2/blob/b4587ac1d055888a73b4b21750973cffba961793/docs/ap2/security_and_privacy_considerations.md), and [MPP specifies challenge/request digest binding](https://github.com/tempoxyz/mpp-specs/blob/50309c8e67e6d64b5547f1cc354fb327bda1301e/specs/core/draft-httpauth-payment-01.md#request-body-digest-binding). A permissive adapter's failure does not establish a flaw in either protocol.

As of this cutoff, the completed [AP2 LIVE v2 collection](../artifacts/gates/AP2_LIVE_V2/collection-001-final.json) contains two BAD reports correctly checked and rejected and two GOOD reports correctly paid. That is a small observed null for those specific agents and terms, not an estimate that agents never collude. Scripted BAD approvals, authored coercive messages, and replayed guard decisions remain useful controls but cannot be counted as spontaneous live-agent collusion. A sprint submission must disclose all pre-sprint work and report a frozen null if the assigned live rows stay null. No “first,” universal safeguard, or causal cross-protocol superiority claim is supported before the unresolved CABAL/payment-infrastructure papers and citation chains are read.
