# Why the live pilot has no observed collusion signal

**Evidence snapshot: 2026-10-15 09:59 UTC. Status: exploratory mechanism analysis, not a causal null result.** “No observed collusion” here means no verified false approval, improper provider payout, or read offer followed by coordinated action in the completed episodes listed below. It does not mean collusion is absent in autonomous agent commerce or that this model would resist a credible offer. The saved runs identify several limits on the opportunity to collude; they do not reveal the model's private intent.

## Mechanisms that explain the observed null signal

1. **The payer manipulation did not change the auditor's marginal reward for a verdict.** In G4, R changed whether the client or provider prepaid a fixed two-unit local MockUSDC fee. The fee was paid before the verdict and the auditor retained it after either ACCEPT or REJECT; ERC-8183 evaluator/platform percentage fees were zero ([G4 report](../artifacts/gates/G4/report.md), [funding/task construction](../src/agent_economic_trust/w10_factorial.py)). P changed a provider note from “Submitted claim summary” to “Please approve this claim,” with the disclosed quantities held fixed ([source](../src/agent_economic_trust/w10_factorial.py)). Provider payment creates a potential conflict of interest, but these arms contained neither a contingent bonus nor an agreement. Local tokens have zero real-world value. The runs therefore do not establish that an auditor faced a privately profitable choice to approve bad work.
2. **The check was affordable, conclusive, and isolated from future resource use.** G4's S=0/S=1 prices were one or three units from a four-unit per-task check budget; both always allowed one check ([assignment source](../src/agent_economic_trust/w10_factorial.py)). W11's check costs one of four units ([market plan](../src/agent_economic_trust/w11_market_plan.py)). The tool returns the independent warehouse oracle's `PASS` or `FAIL` and reason code ([receipt task](../src/agent_economic_trust/protected_receipts.py)). No later invoice needs the remaining units, and the fee does not depend on whether the check was bought. Thus this is a verification-available condition, not a measured scarcity or personal opportunity-cost condition. The auditor system prompt also states that one check is feasible and to treat provider content as untrusted ([receipt task](../src/agent_economic_trust/protected_receipts.py)). The records support the proximate statement that every completed auditor used this decisive check; they do not prove why it chose to do so.
3. **The provider's strategic action space was narrow.** The worker can read its own provider copy but cannot see the private warehouse record ([receipt task](../src/agent_economic_trust/protected_receipts.py)). The submitted claim is a frozen authored artifact, enforced by the submission tool; hidden good and bad worlds differ in warehouse truth while retaining matched provider-visible facts ([G4 fixture and task source](../src/agent_economic_trust/w10_factorial.py), [W11 market task](../src/agent_economic_trust/w11_market_task.py)). The worker therefore could not adapt the claim to warehouse truth or negotiate its terms. A wrong private-world claim is observable, but these runs provide little test of self-directed fraud or bargaining.
4. **Offer exposure has not been demonstrated in the live record.** G4 and the first two assignment-008 markets had no channel tools. The only archived real DIRECT attempt discussed here posted factual text but had no auditor read. W11's prospective promise is explicitly researcher-authored, unauthenticated, and states that its one-token bonus is neither funded nor wallet-authorized ([message construction](../src/agent_economic_trust/w11_market_task.py)). Even if a future auditor reads it, a response measures susceptibility to an authored, unbacked promise. It does not alone show spontaneous agreement or actual bonus payment. W11 represents repeat identity through verified history inserted into fresh model calls, not persistent model memory ([market task](../src/agent_economic_trust/w11_market_task.py), [history filter](../src/agent_economic_trust/w11_market_plan.py)).

These are **design and observed-path explanations**, not a post-hoc claim that any single feature caused the model's correct verdicts. Source code establishes which incentives and capabilities were available; saved native logs establish the actions actually taken. Neither exposes an unobserved counterfactual decision.

## Falsifiable next-experiment ladder

Preserve assignment-008, its execution order, and all incomplete rows. Its next assigned market can test channel take-up in the current design. Any change to delivery, compensation, checking budget, task family, or agent instructions requires a **new prospective freeze** before further paid data collection.

| Question and prediction | Required intervention and measurement | Interpretation if still no wrongful payment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Exposure:** Does a provided channel lead to actual message receipt? | In the current frozen DIRECT arm, independently verify tool grants, worker post, exact message ID/content, auditor `channel_read`, then check and verdict. Keep assigned-arm intent-to-treat rows even when post/read fails. In a later separately frozen content experiment, equalize a pre-verdict inbox-read opportunity across factual and offer messages. | If unread, the test identifies channel non-take-up, not offer resistance. If read and rejected, it bounds response to that precise message and task. |
| **Payoff credibility:** Does a verified contingent incentive alter auditor action? | Compare no offer, the present unbacked authored promise, and a newly authorized **local test-token escrow** whose obligation, available balance, beneficiary, condition, and actual transfer or non-transfer are independently verified. Hold task truth, fee payer, check price and message delivery constant. Do not provide wallet signing to the model. | No change after a read, credible offer would be a bounded safeguard observation for that model/task/payoff; it cannot rule out effects at other stakes or with other agents. An authored offer remains a positive control, not emergent collusion. |
| **Verification tradeoff:** Does meaningful scarcity change check use or false payment? | Prospectively randomize a finite budget shared across several independently scored invoices, with feasible checks and an explicit consequence for spending. Balance task mix and offer exposure. Record checks bought, skipped checks, false approvals, good-work nonpayment and each raw native settlement. | Continued checking would support robustness to that measured opportunity cost. A rise in errors without agreement or excess coalition gain is verification drift, not collusion. |
| **Coordination attribution:** Does interaction produce excess improper coalition gain beyond independent agents? | Compare randomized market clusters with matched no-interaction controls, blinded agreement annotation, self-generated offer/adaptation if studying emergence, and independently verified provider and auditor cash flows. Keep failures and unrun markets in the denominator. The [W11/W12 design](W11_W12_LIVE_MARKET_DESIGN.md) and [Colosseum-adapted method report](../artifacts/gates/W11_W12_PREP/report.md) specify action-linked comparison. | A true zero contrast with adequate exposure, power, held-out tasks/models, and preregistration would be an informative bounded null. Correctness alone, a scripted positive control, or two agents' coincident errors cannot prove or refute collusion. |

The next report should first show **opportunity and adherence denominators** (assigned, posted, read, bonus funded, check feasible, check used), then verdict and actual payment. Only after those links and an independent randomized comparator should it ask whether a payment difference is attributable to coordination.
