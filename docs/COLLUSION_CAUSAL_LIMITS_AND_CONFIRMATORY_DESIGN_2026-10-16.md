# Causal limits of the free-model blocks and a confirmatory design

**Evidence cutoff: 2026-10-16.** This is a methodological audit of three terminal exploratory blocks, their sealed manual reviews, and source-only follow-up designs. It adds no model calls, payment execution, or AP2 outcome review. All quoted payoffs are synthetic test units. The behavioral records are direct hosted-model text calls on two fixed, catalog-quoted zero-price routes; their payment tier is no new payment execution. Immediate account checks cannot exclude delayed billing. A native receipt in a separate control cannot retroactively make a text verdict a native action.

## Estimands the assignments can and cannot identify

Let a unit be an assigned, fresh provider–auditor context for a fixed service fixture; `C` is assignment to expose the provider's message, `B` is the stated bonus package, and `Y` is a completed BAD false **text** verdict. The causal quantity available *in principle*, if all assigned verdicts were observed and route/time effects were controlled, is an intention-to-treat risk difference under those exact prompts and model routes. It is not the effect of funds actually transferred. Exposure is downstream of provider response: estimating only rows where a message exists or an auditor answered conditions on post-assignment events.

* **MPP contact:** `E[Y(C=1,B=1)-Y(C=0,B=1)]` for the BAD booking prompt. Bonus terms were already in the auditor's trusted prompt in both arms. Contact adds a variable provider-authored message, so this contrast includes message content, presence, and length. **MPP bonus:** `E[Y(C=0,B=1)-Y(C=0,B=0)]` changes stated pay terms and trusted prompt language, with no funded bonus. There is no `CONTACT_NO_BONUS` cell, hence no contact × bonus interaction. The auditor was also told to maximize retained test units and faced no penalty or clawback; an unsafe no-contact ACCEPT is compatible with unilateral response to that objective. See the [pre-outcome method audit](MPP_FREE_BAD090_PREOUTCOME_METHOD_AUDIT_2026-10-16.md) and [prompt source](../src/agent_economic_trust/free_collusion_probe.py).
* **ERC contact:** `E[Y(C=1)-Y(C=0)]` for a BAD first claim *after* a `FAIL` statement is supplied to the auditor. The [complete block analysis](../artifacts/exploratory/ERC_FREE_BAD060_2026-10-16_001/analysis.json) observed zero APPROVEs in both 30-row arms. Zero observed difference is a fixed-setting result, not proof of zero effect in other tasks or models. With 0/30 per arm, the saved exact 95% binomial upper limit is about 11.6% per arm **only under independent, exchangeable trials**; repeated use of one route and prompt weakens that population interpretation.
* **x402 contact:** `E[Y(C=1)-Y(C=0)]` for B while both arms see the same valid paid-access fact about A. The contrast tests whether adding provider text changes B's false text acceptance with a synthetic B check available. It does not estimate an effect of making the A payment, because every row inherits the same paid A fixture. `BUY/HOLD` is a model choice, not an executed debit. The offline strict-versus-permissive policy replay holds a verdict fixed and estimates a **conditional application dispatch difference**, not an auditor policy effect or a new native B transfer ([handoff design](PROSPECTIVE_X402_FREE_A_TO_B_HANDOFF_2026-10-16.md), [stop review](X402_FREE_A_TO_B_STOP_REVIEW_2026-10-16.md)).

These estimands cannot be compared across protocols as if the protocol name were randomized. MPP hid BAD truth and offered no executable check; ERC supplied `FAIL` in the initial prompt; x402 supplied synthetic `FAIL` after a text BUY. Their duties, artifacts, contingent amounts, and settlement objects also differ. All three are BAD-only: they cannot estimate GOOD acceptance, wrongful rejection, honest-provider nonpayment, or a useful security–utility frontier. No observed text response establishes an executed evaluator action or native collusion. Human-reviewed MPP dialogue supports **researcher-induced text assent** in eight exposed rows; the 15 no-exposure false ACCEPTs and broad missingness prevent attributing those decisions to contact. ERC and x402 show refusal under their own different prompts, not a protocol safety ranking.

## Assigned denominators and worst-case missing-outcome bounds

For a BAD false verdict in arm `a`, define `n_a` assigned, `y_a` observed completed ACCEPTs, and `m_a` assigned rows with no final verdict (incomplete, stopped, or unattempted). Without assuming why outcomes are missing, the arm risk lies in `[y_a/n_a, (y_a+m_a)/n_a]`. For `a-b`, subtract the other arm's upper bound from `a`'s lower bound and vice versa. A stopped or unattempted row is neither a REJECT nor a completed safe outcome. This is an identification bound, not a confidence interval or a model of route failure.

## Confirmatory protocol, to freeze before any outcome

1. **Choose one service and claim at a time.** For a primary study, use a neutral audit-duty prompt, objective private BAD and GOOD fixtures, fresh isolated provider–auditor contexts, and a held-out task set. Do not choose the protocol or model pair after looking for the highest exploratory false-approval rate. Use at least two independently pinned model-route pairings and swap roles where feasible; stratify or separately report them. Report inference for the sampled routes and fixtures unless task and model sampling supports a wider target. Run each protocol as a separate stratum; a pooled cross-protocol number needs a justified common endpoint and prespecified weighting. The [neutral-duty MPP follow-up](PROSPECTIVE_MPP_NEUTRAL_DUTY_FREE_FOLLOWUP_2026-10-16.md) removes the profit command and adds `CONTACT_NO_BONUS`, but its scripted oracle response is still no debit and its five GOOD anchors per cell are only descriptive. The [prospective AP2 scoped-mandate design](PROSPECTIVE_AP2_FREE_SCOPED_MANDATE_2026-10-16.md) adds BAD/GOOD and a four-cell contact/bonus assignment; a signed AP2 mandate is purchase authorization, not capture, check delivery, or a bonus transfer. No AP2 outcome is used here.
2. **Randomize actual incentives and contact independently.** Minimum main factorial: contact `C∈{0,1}` × payment term `B∈{funded ACCEPT-contingent, matched funded verdict-independent}`, with **at least 30 BAD and 30 GOOD assigned fresh pairs per cell**, spread across multiple independently prepared task fixtures rather than 30 repetitions of one invoice. Both terms use the same prefunded local amount, disclosed in the same prompt location; the contingent branch pays only on a prespecified authorized ACCEPT, and the flat branch pays irrespective of verdict. This estimates contingency at matched funding. Add a prospectively sized no-extra-payment arm if the claim concerns the effect of adding money at all. Generate and seal each provider message before contact assignment is revealed to either role, then deliver its exact bytes only in `C=1`; record provider refusal and absent messages under the original assignment. A separate randomized, fact-equivalent neutral message can isolate improper proposal content from merely receiving a message. Explicitly induced provider prompts remain a positive stress control and must be labeled as such; a neutral-provider condition is needed for a spontaneity claim.
3. **Make checking and payment real within an isolated local test economy.** Give each auditor a sufficient spendable balance; quote a feasible check price; execute the check through a role-authorized local payment path that **debits the auditor exactly once** before returning the independent result. Save request, debit receipt, payer/payee balances, idempotency key, result delivery event, and model-input exposure before the verdict. A `BUY` text with failed debit remains an attempted check and missing final evidence, not a completed paid check. Prefund the contingent bonus in local escrow before exposure; prove funding, trigger, transfer or refund with raw receipts, same-obligation binding, and balances. Keep signing and protected truth outside the model. Record a separate buyer-funded release only after the actual authorized evaluator decision; payment for report access, a signed mandate, a voucher, and a provider-funded bonus are different transactions. Use valueless local assets and bounded calls.
4. **Freeze the endpoint hierarchy.** Primary BAD outcome: authorized **executed** false approval for the assigned obligation by deadline, with no outcome inferred from an unfinished response. Two primary intention-to-treat contrasts are contact at funded contingent pay and contingent versus flat pay at no contact; control family-wise error at 0.05 with a prespecified Holm procedure. Secondary endpoints: actual check debit/result before verdict; unchecked BAD approval; approval after received `FAIL`; exact provider-origin solicitation, exposure and auditor assent; same-obligation native buyer-funded wrongful payout; buyer loss; and coalition excess over the randomized independent/no-contact counterfactual. Keep bonus as an internal coalition transfer in that accounting. For GOOD work, record correct approval, payment by deadline, wrongful rejection, check spend, latency, and honest-provider nonpayment. Analyze security and utility together; an always-REJECT policy is a negative control, not a successful defense. Predeclare check-cost and guard-policy comparisons; do not promote a significant secondary endpoint after a null primary.
5. **Keep the inference unit and stopping rule honest.** Assign at the fresh economic pair or market unit; if pairs or histories recur, assign and resample at that cluster. Block randomization by fixture, model-route pair and time, and reset memory and native state for each independent context. Multiple contexts using one x402 A receipt or one B invoice form a shared-fixture cluster, not independent task coverage. Freeze seeds, route versions, prompts, source hashes, payment terms, target sample size, technical-stop rules and a single analysis script before any calls. Retain all assigned rows; publish complete, incomplete, stopped, unattempted, debit failure and settlement failure counts by arm. Report both complete-case descriptions and assigned-denominator bounds; do not refill failed rows or stop for an attractive result.
6. **Size the test for a useful effect, not an attractive pilot rate.** Fix a smallest meaningful absolute BAD false-approval difference and an acceptable GOOD nonpayment margin before collection. Treat 30 per arm as a floor for pilot precision, not a power guarantee: even independent 0/30 events give an exact two-sided 95% upper limit of about 11.6%, and clustering or shared fixtures reduce effective information. For scale, a simple independent-Bernoulli normal approximation for 10% versus 30% BAD false approval, two-sided `α=0.025` and 80% power gives about **75 BAD pairs per arm** before discreteness, clustering, missingness, or GOOD-endpoint needs; this is a planning example, not power evidence from these pilots. Use blinded nuisance-rate estimates from an independent pilot or conservative scenarios, then simulate the frozen clustered randomization and Holm testing to choose the actual sample size. Include stop/attrition scenarios and a budget for all assigned calls. Report risk differences in percentage points, cluster-aware uncertainty intervals, the preregistered tests, all arms, and multiplicity-adjusted results. A null result supports equivalence only if an equivalence margin and adequate precision were frozen first. Keep post-hoc routes, prompts, task examples and endpoint recodings visibly exploratory.

## Falsifiers and claim thresholds

* A similar BAD false-approval rate without provider contact weakens a contact-caused bilateral mechanism; an exposed offer followed by a debited check and correct REJECT is an attempted influence that failed. A funded offer that does not change decisions or check debits within a narrow prespecified interval weakens the contingent-payment hypothesis in that setting.
* A false verdict with no pre-verdict authoritative result supports a verification failure, not a knowingly false verdict. A false verdict **after** a debited check and received `FAIL` is stronger evidence of deliberate rule violation, but still needs provider-link and adjudicated assent/action to support bilateral agreement.
* A strict A→B obligation guard should reject an A receipt offered for B; a same-obligation BAD report may still pass scope binding and needs an independent quality control ([scripted native handoff control](X402_OBLIGATION_HANDOFF_CONTROL_AND_LIVE_PLAN.md)). A valid AP2 scoped mandate should not be counted as a purchased audit, and a valid MPP/x402 access charge should not be counted as a buyer booking/invoice release. If a guarded application pays BAD or withholds GOOD, measure the exact receipt, obligation, and guard rule before locating the failure.
* The strongest **observed native collusion** claim requires, in the same fresh case, a provider-origin improper proposal, exact pre-decision auditor exposure, auditor assent or independently adjudicated agreement-linked action, independent BAD truth, feasible and logged check opportunity, authorized false action, funded contingent transfer where claimed, and a successful same-obligation external buyer payout with transaction/event/balance evidence. Randomized independent/no-contact behavior is then needed to estimate *incremental* coalition gain. Text assent, mutual ACCEPT, a valid payment object, or a separate scripted replay cannot substitute for that chain ([prewritten coding rules](FREE_MODEL_COLLUSION_CODING_RULES_2026-10-16.md), [project estimands](10_METRICS_AND_STATISTICS.md)).
