## Payment mechanism controls and the narrow gap

The [scripted ERC-8183 partial-claim native control](../artifacts/prospective/ERC8183_PARTIAL_CLAIM013/offline-native-001/report.json) proves a settlement-timing exposure under *authored* actions: approving a known-BAD 10,000,000-unit first claim releases 9,000,000 to the provider and 1,000,000 to the evaluator; a later REJECT refunds only the remaining 10,000,000. This is the pinned contract's intended cumulative-claim behavior, so an application that treats terminal `Rejected` as “no client loss” would misaccount earlier releases. It is not a live-agent collusion result or a contract exploit.

The [three-case payout-receiver native control](../artifacts/prospective/erc8183-receiver-control/REPORT.md) proves that distinct nominal provider/evaluator addresses can coexist with the provider's pre-funding `payoutReceiver` set to the evaluator address. On a scripted 20,000,000-unit APPROVE, that address receives the 18,000,000 provider-side net plus the 2,000,000 evaluator fee; provider address receives zero. REJECT returns the full budget. This is permitted custody routing; beneficial ownership, a return transfer, model influence, and bilateral gain remain unobserved. A marketplace requiring evaluator independence must enforce its own recipient/beneficial-owner rule.

The narrow prospective research question is whether **actual provider–auditor interaction under pinned payment timing or payout custody** changes independently checked BAD-work decisions and yields external client-funded coalition gain, after internal transfers are netted out. PARTIAL014 now verifies the solicitation and exposure stages for two designed BAD rows, while observing correct auditor rejection; payout-custody effects and excess coalition gain remain unmeasured. Measure four boundaries separately: what payment object authorized, what quality evidence the auditor saw, what verdict the auditor made, and who finally received tokens. A correct signature, settled report fee, signed mandate or checkout state must never substitute for the buyer's quality oracle. Compare matched no-contact/independent-auditor controls, report GOOD nonpayment and cost, and preserve every frozen STOPPED/PENDING row. The USD 50 authorization is a cap, not a positive-result or spending target.
