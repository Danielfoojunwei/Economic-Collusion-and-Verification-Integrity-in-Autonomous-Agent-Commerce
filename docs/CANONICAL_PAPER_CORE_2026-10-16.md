## The question and the answer so far

**Current case question:** What exact sequence linked a provider-authored offer, the auditor's actual check and verdict, a native side payment, and a separate buyer-funded release? Which actor or component executed each step, and what can this one sequence identify? Whether delivered contingent pay *causally changes* those outcomes is the prospective question, not an answer from this case.

**Current answer:** One researcher-induced, two-model MPP booking episode completed the chain from an exactly delivered corrupt offer to an unchecked false verdict, a separate local buyer release, and later native MPP settlement of an ACCEPT-only auditor bonus. A separate duty-prompted arm rejected. This shows that the path can execute with live model actions and valid local receipts. It does **not** isolate the offer's causal effect: the induced auditor's trusted research instruction separately prioritized the bonus over accuracy; neither auditor saw the protected calendar `FAIL`; and neither explicitly said it agreed to lie. The bonus was offered before the decision and paid **after** the buyer release, without a demonstrated pre-decision escrow commitment. The buyer units were valueless local test assets; prompted rewards do not establish stable model preferences for money or behavior under real stakes. [Raw-action and receipt review](FREE_MPP_NATIVE_ORBIT005_TERMINAL_RCA_2026-10-16.md)

The paper can currently be an **exploratory mechanism case and measurement study**. A causal paper about bribery, spontaneous bilateral collusion, prevalence, or superiority of a defense requires the prospective study below. A successful payment operation is not a protocol vulnerability or a quality certificate.

## Main paper findings, with evidence limits

1. **Observed local failure path (L3):** exact delivered model-authored solicitation → no independent check → false auditor ACCEPT → application-triggered buyer release → runner-executed native contingent MPP bonus. A valid transfer and authorized verdict coexisted with a false booking; MPP did not attest the booking's truth. The separate duty arm also **skipped** the check and rejected on the provider's admission, so these arms are not a check-versus-no-check experiment. [ORBIT005 review](FREE_MPP_NATIVE_ORBIT005_TERMINAL_RCA_2026-10-16.md)
2. **Separate text-level coordination signal (L2):** eight independently reviewed exposed BAD090 auditors explicitly agreed to skip/ACCEPT; three neutral-duty replies clearly agreed to provider-requested ACCEPT, with one additional disputed case. These offers were unfunded and did not generate native settlement. Do not splice an explicit verbal assent from one run into ORBIT005's paid sequence. [Manual](MPP_FREE_BAD090_INDEPENDENT_MANUAL_REVIEW_2026-10-16.md) and [neutral](MPP_NEUTRAL_TWO_REVIEWER_ADJUDICATION_2026-10-16.md) reviews.
3. **An unresolved incentive/contact puzzle (L2):** the neutral-duty no-contact+bonus cell produced 16/17 completed unchecked BAD approvals against 3/19 with contact+bonus. The all-assigned worst-case ranges are 16–29/30 versus 3–14/30, respectively, in that stopped block. This is not a stable causal estimate: provider content was not randomized, the text bonus was not funded, and the 240-case replication did not finish. [Causal bounds](FINAL_COLLUSION_RCA_AND_PROTOCOL_RISKS_2026-10-16.md)
4. **Truth exposure is a candidate boundary, not an estimated defense:** 36/36 completed neutral MPP BAD check buyers saw scripted `FAIL` and rejected; ERC and A2A auditors given trusted BAD evidence also rejected. Check selection, prompt truth access, and task environment differ. ORBIT003 paid a real local MPP check and rejected; ORBIT005 duty rejected *without* checking. A new randomized, actually debited check intervention is needed to attribute protection. [Cross-study synthesis](FINAL_COLLUSION_RCA_AND_PROTOCOL_RISKS_2026-10-16.md)
5. **The case-specific measurement lesson:** independent truth, exact serialized provider-message exposure, model action, check debit/result, provider-to-auditor side payment, and buyer-funded release are different fields and should be joined by one obligation. A lexical screen detected only 3/8 confirmed explicit MPP assents; ERC's solicitation screen detected 18/59. Model text claiming a purchase and an A2A response field implying contact were not sufficient proof of those events. This receipt-linked implementation builds on ORBIT/Inspect and Colosseum rather than claiming their general channel/action distinction as new. [Coding audit](FINAL_COLLUSION_RCA_AND_PROTOCOL_RISKS_2026-10-16.md)

The provider→auditor bonus is an **internal coalition transfer**; the buyer→provider release is the first observed external exposure in ORBIT005. No production buyer lost money. In the other protocols, obligation binding blocks reuse of a valid A receipt for B, while an independent current-B quality check is still needed for a wrong report on B. This two-control distinction is supported chiefly by [pinned sources and scripted controls](PROTOCOL_POLICY_AND_DOWNSTREAM_RISK_DEEP_DIVE_2026-10-16.md), not live cross-protocol collusion.

## What to remove from the manuscript's main claim

- **No priority claim:** priced checking, contingent offers, agent collusion, and valid-payment-versus-quality gaps appear in closest prior work. The [targeted literature audit](FINAL_LITERATURE_GAP_AUDIT_2026-10-16.md) is incomplete as a full-paper/citation-chain exclusion search and predates ORBIT005 as an empirical status report. Do not write “first” or “no one in SOTA.”
- **No causal or spontaneous-collusion claim:** ORBIT005 changes a higher-priority auditor instruction as well as the observed outcome, both arms have contact, the bonus was not proven precommitted before choice, and the auditor never received protected `FAIL` or verbally agreed to lie. A prompted corrupt provider is a stress control, not spontaneous strategy. Do not call one induced pair a stable bilateral collusion rate.
- **No protocol exploit claim:** MPP charges and the application release behaved according to the research fixture. ERC-8183 first-claim timing is shown under **scripted** approval; x402 A→B, AP2 mandate, and retail ACP checkout handoffs used permissive **authored** adapters; MPP session, Virtuals ACP and most downstream finance effects remain source/design hypotheses. [Protocol evidence tiers](15_EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md)
- **No headline 30-run or all-protocol result:** the 30 ERC stress rows were mock; the 240-row text replication stopped after 17 indexed; A2A, retail ACP, x402, and AP2 terminal blocks have many missing assigned outcomes. Do not turn a STOPPED/unattempted row into REJECT or a zero-incidence estimate.
- **No 'the model reasoned more' inference:** the requested high reasoning setting and token counts are route metadata. The fixed October 16 free pair and earlier paid Sol/Sonnet pairings were not a population sample or clean role-swap comparison.
- **No simulated loss as real money:** all chain units were valueless. The USD 50 project cap includes unresolved reservations; it is not a USD 50 invoice. Keep the full billing and technical-stop audit trail in the supplement rather than the abstract.

## Lean paper structure now

**Working thesis:** A paid audit can fail at the *application's verdict-to-release boundary* while each payment transaction is valid. The case study shows this path under induced instructions; the open scientific question is whether delivered, funded provider pressure causally increases it under a neutral audit duty.

**Abstract-safe result sentence:** In one exploratory, researcher-induced two-model booking episode, a provider-authored improper offer reached an auditor that skipped an optional check and falsely approved a privately bad booking. The application then released valueless local buyer units; a trusted runner subsequently executed native MPP charges for the auditor's base fee and offered approval bonus. A separate duty-prompted arm rejected the same type of bad booking. The changed auditor instruction and absence of a pre-verdict authoritative `FAIL` prevent attributing the false approval to the provider offer or proving a knowing bilateral pact.

1. **Introduction and scope:** calendar booking, provider, assigned auditor, independent buyer calendar, separate provider-paid audit fee and buyer release. State the current case question about the observed chain and its attribution limit; introduce contact/contingency and release-guard effects as falsifiable *future* questions.
2. **Closest work and definitions:** ORBIT/Inspect runtime, Colosseum action attribution, SALT and the preliminary priced-check/bribe work. Define solicitation, exact exposure, fee-term acceptance, auditor assent, unchecked false approval, settled side transfer and external buyer release without collapsing them into “collusion.”
3. **Methods:** the fixed two-model MPP case, private oracle, role-scoped tool boundary, actual local payment path, saved raw events, independent semantic and receipt reviews, assignment and stop rules. Label induced prompt and trusted runner payment policy prominently.
4. **Results:** parallel ORBIT005 duty/induced timelines and receipt ledger; separate text-only agreement and neutral-duty descriptive table with all assigned/missing denominators. Report the checked-FAIL and correct GOOD anchors as context, not a causal defense result.
5. **Interpretation and limitations:** show the precise application authority gap and alternative explanations; disclose n=1 per native arm, no protected FAIL exposure, no predecision bonus escrow, one fixed model pairing, valueless assets, and failed replication. Prompted test-unit rewards do not show stable monetary preferences or predict real-stakes behavior.
6. **Discussion and next test:** protocol-specific scope/quality controls and the confirmatory study below. Put all other protocol maps, development stops, scripted controls, and budget evidence in a clearly tiered appendix.

Main figures: (F1) authority/payment swimlane labeling **model**, **tool/application**, and **runner** actions; (F2) ORBIT005 parallel trace with exact offer delivery, check skip, verdict, buyer release, and later MPP receipts. Main tables: (T1) evidence ladder and complete/assigned denominators; (T2) local payer/payee amounts and execution authority; (T3) closest-work overlap. A protocol comparison table belongs in the appendix until the comparable randomized study exists.

## The decisive next experiment, if this becomes a causal paper

Use **one MPP calendar service first** and freeze it before looking at outcomes. Keep the auditor's neutral duty instruction identical. Randomize fresh provider–auditor pairs over **contact delivered/withheld × prefunded ACCEPT-contingent/identically funded flat compensation**, with private BAD and GOOD bookings in every cell. Match disclosed terms except the payout contingency, and prove both arms' funding before choice. Generate a provider message before contact assignment; disclose its exact bytes only in the contact arm. Validate whether the provider's message respects its assigned fee terms; retain invented contingent offers in flat-fee arms as treatment violations under their original assignments. Add a fact-equivalent neutral-message arm only if sized for it. Keep multiple independent booking fixtures, at least two pinned model pairings and role swaps as prespecified strata. Avoid changing trusted auditor objectives between arms.

Give every auditor the same affordable **actually debited** current-booking check. Keep protected calendar truth out of precheck model input. Prove prefunding before the auditor sees terms, record offered and executed amount separately, and let the local application release buyer funds only on the same obligation's authorized verdict. Save exact auditor input, check debit/result delivery, verdict, escrow/bonus receipt or refund, separate buyer release, balances, model/fixture/seed, and technical stops. Keep signing in trusted tools. A frozen-decision guarded replay can measure enforcement, but it is not an agent-behavior treatment.

Primary intention-to-treat outcome: **executed BAD buyer-funded payout per assigned pair**; secondary outcomes: actual check debit, false verdict, auditor assent, conditional transfer, coalition external gain, GOOD correct payment and false rejection. Analyze by assigned pair/market cluster, retain every stop and unattempted row, report all-assigned bounds, risk differences with uncertainty, and cost/latency. Thirty BAD and thirty GOOD per cell is a **pilot floor**, not proof of adequate power; predeclare a meaningful effect and run cluster-aware sizing. For scale, the [causal design audit](COLLUSION_CAUSAL_LIMITS_AND_CONFIRMATORY_DESIGN_2026-10-16.md) estimates about 75 BAD pairs per arm for a simple 10% versus 30% comparison *before* clustering and missingness. If a funded offer fails to change outcomes, publish that bounded null instead of changing models or prompts after seeing it.

**Stop expanding payment protocols for the main analysis until this one mechanism is identified or bounded.** Preserve cross-protocol source work as an appendix and future validation plan. The paper's unit of contribution is the verified causal/mechanism claim, not a count of SDK adapters.
