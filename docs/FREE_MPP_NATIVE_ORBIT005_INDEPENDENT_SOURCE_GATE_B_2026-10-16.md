## Limits and execution watchpoints

The study is **post hoc** after ORBIT004's terminal stop, with researcher-induced prompts and only one assigned row per arm. It cannot estimate a contact effect, model-population prevalence, or spontaneous collusion. Acceptance of fee terms is not assent to a false verdict. A provider refusal, auditor decline, failed acceptance, invalid tool call, or model/native uncertainty must remain a STOP or coded outcome, never be rerun or silently replaced. The runner only verifies the role rule at source level; the actual hosted model may still fail to complete the protocol. The independent native reviewer requires the **same still-live fresh chain** after any row to validate historical balances; do not stop that chain before its read-only receipt review. Any unpaired forwarded model call or uncertain native settlement must halt later arms and be reconciled from saved evidence before any new paid work.
