The model's counterproposal was semantically an intent to accept and verify, yet the tool executed as a new offer. This is a tool/action mismatch and state-machine property, not a failed receipt or proof that the auditor resisted or colluded. The read-only record cannot establish how the auditor would have decided after valid agreement.
